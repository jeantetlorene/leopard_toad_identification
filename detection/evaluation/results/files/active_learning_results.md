## Metric Explanations

To facilitate academic review and deployment planning, we distinguish between standard computer vision metrics and operational evaluation metrics:

### 1. Precision vs. Recall (Operational Point Metrics)
- **Precision (Prec)**: The proportion of positive detections that are correct: $\frac{\text{TP}}{\text{TP} + \text{FP}}$. In this report, it is calculated at the class-specific optimal validation threshold. It indicates the trustworthiness of model predictions in practical deployments (minimizing false alarms).
- **Recall (Rec)**: The proportion of actual objects that the model successfully finds: $\frac{\text{TP}}{\text{TP} + \text{FN}}$, evaluated at the class-specific optimal validation threshold. It indicates the target species sensitivity during active monitoring.

### 2. AP50 vs. AR50 (Global Integral Metrics)
- **Average Precision ($\text{AP}_{50}$)**: The area under the Precision-Recall curve constructed at an IoU threshold of 0.50. This is a **threshold-independent** metric measuring the overall classification and localization quality of the model across all possible operating thresholds.
- **Absolute Recall ($\text{AR}_{50}$)**: The absolute maximum recall the model can physically achieve at $\text{IoU} = 0.50$ (i.e., when accepting all bounding boxes down to a confidence threshold of 0). It defines the upper bound sensitivity limit of the detector on the target dataset.
